Time Detectives: Legacies of Civilisations

Ancient artifacts are vanishing across history. As a time-travelling detective, you'll investigate thirty-six cases across civilisations and historical periods, examine real evidence, and reason your way to the truth — one case at a time.

Your mission

Each case drops you into a moment in history where an artifact — or the true story behind it — has gone missing. You'll gather evidence, weigh conflicting accounts, and make a judgement call. How you reason matters more than getting lucky.

  • Investigate civilisations from Ancient Mesopotamia to the Melaka Sultanate
  • Practise real historical thinking skills: sourcing, corroboration, close reading, and more
  • Earn points for sound reasoning, and add a legacy artifact to your trophy cabinet for every case you solve perfectly

Class settings

Only the admin can change these two settings.

  • Only signed-in pupils, and only for a case they have already finished.
  • The AI sees this case's notes only, so it can't be used as a general chatbot.
  • Questions are capped at 200 characters and obvious "ignore your rules" tricks are refused.
  • Every question and answer is logged below, and pupils can report an answer to you.
  • Nothing the AI does changes points, skill scores or the leaderboard.
